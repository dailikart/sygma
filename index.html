FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which inverters and data loggers do you support?

Sygma Solar integrates with 40+ inverter and logger manufacturers through cloud APIs, Modbus TCP/RTU and MQTT. If your device isn't listed, our gateway can usually connect it within a few days.

How long does onboarding take?

Most customers are live within 1–2 weeks. Plant connections typically take a day; importing customers, projects and inventory is handled by our onboarding team.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted on enterprise cloud infrastructure with daily backups, role-based access control and full audit logs.

Can my customers see their own plant data?

Absolutely. Every plan includes a customer portal and mobile app. Enterprise customers can white-label it with their own brand and domain.

Can I integrate with my existing ERP or accounting software?

Sygma Solar provides a REST API and webhooks, plus ready-made connectors for popular accounting and ERP tools. Our team can build custom integrations on Enterprise plans.

What happens after the free trial?

You can choose a plan that fits your portfolio. If you decide not to continue, you can export all your data — no lock-in.
