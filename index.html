FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Sygma Solar Suite suitable for small installers?

Yes. The Starter plan is built for small teams doing a handful of rooftop projects a month. You can switch on more modules — design, inventory, O&M — as you grow.

How long does it take to get started?

Most customers are live within 7 days. Our onboarding team imports your existing data, configures your workflows and trains your team.

Which inverters can I monitor?

We integrate with most popular inverter and data-logger brands through their cloud APIs, as well as Modbus TCP/RTU and MQTT. If a device isn't supported yet, our team can usually add it quickly.

Does it support subsidy and net-metering workflows?

Yes. Project templates include stages and document checklists for subsidy applications, DISCOM approvals and net-metering, so nothing gets missed.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily, and protected by role-based access control, two-factor authentication and full audit logs.

Can I use it in my own brand?

Proposals and customer apps carry your logo and colours on every plan. Enterprise customers can fully white-label the apps with their own name and domain.

What happens after the free trial?

Pick the plan that suits you. If you decide not to continue, you can export all of your data — there's no lock-in.
