Project workflow

From site survey to subsidy — every step, every document

An invoice creates the project. Each stage goes to the right person — surveyor, store, driver, installer, EB liaison — who finds it in My Tasks. A stage cannot be closed until its photos, serial numbers and documents are in.

  • Survey → dispatch → delivery → installation → DCR → EB / net meter → subsidy
  • Delivery photo, panel and inverter serials, PCR and DCR uploads required
  • Stock leaves the store on dispatch and stays "in transit" until delivered
  • Customer status page and WhatsApp updates at each milestone

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Sygma Solar Suite suitable for small installers?

Yes. You get only the modules you need — for example leads, quotations, billing and projects — and more can be switched on later as you grow, without moving your data.

Does it handle PM Surya Ghar subsidy, DCR and net-metering?

Yes. Subsidy projects get the DCR and subsidy stages automatically, the EB / net-meter stage tracks the PCR and approvals, and the subsidy status report shows where every file stands.

Can leads come in automatically?

Yes. Enquiries from IndiaMART, Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads and Google Ads lead forms are added to Leads automatically, with duplicate phone numbers merged.

Does it work with Tally?

Yes. Every invoice, receipt, purchase and expense posts its own voucher, and you can export ledger masters and vouchers as Tally XML for your accountant.

Can my site team use it on their phones?

Yes. It runs in the phone's browser and can be installed on the home screen like an app. Surveyors, drivers and installers see their own tasks and upload photos from the site.

Will invoices and quotations carry my brand?

Yes. Your logo, company details, GST number, signature and seal appear on quotations, invoices and receipts, and you can choose the colour theme.

How long does it take to get started?

Usually a few days. We set up your company, import your items, customers and suppliers from Excel, create logins for your team and walk everyone through their part.
